# Closing & Negotiation

Closing is a schedule, not a magic phrase. Trade, don't cave.

## Mutual Action Plan (the core artifact)

Build a dated table working *backward* from the prospect's go-live need:

| Date | Milestone | Owner (us) | Owner (them) | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Jun 10 | Security review complete | AE | IT lead | ☐ |
| Jun 17 | Legal redlines resolved | — | Legal | ☐ |
| Jun 24 | Signature | — | Econ. buyer | ☐ |
| Jul 01 | Kickoff | CS | Champion | ☐ |

Share it with the champion; a prospect who won't co-own dates is telling you something —
surface that signal to the user.

## Negotiation rules of engagement

- **Know the floor before the call.** Get the user's walk-away terms and approved trade list
  first; never improvise a discount.
- **Every concession buys something:** price ↓ only in exchange for longer term, upfront
  payment, case study, referral, or reduced scope. Name the trade explicitly.
- **Concede in decreasing increments** (10% → 4% → 1%) — signals the floor is near.
- **Silence beats stacking.** After stating price, stop writing. In email drafts: state terms,
  one question, end.
- If procurement demands start conflicting with the champion's timeline, recommend a
  three-way alignment call rather than email ping-pong.

## Closing moves (pick by situation)

- **Summary close:** replay agreed pains → outcomes → terms, ask for the order.
- **Timeline close:** anchor on *their* go-live date and walk the MAP backward.
- **Option close:** "Option B with annual billing, or Option A monthly — which works?"
- Never fake scarcity or deadlines; real quarter-end incentives only if the user confirms them.

## Workflow

1. Intake deal state (MEDDICC score, proposal, open objections) → output: MAP draft, risk list
   (single-threaded? no economic buyer contact?), and this week's two actions.
2. For each negotiation email/call, produce a brief: their likely asks, our trades, walk-away.
3. On verbal yes: draft the confirmation email (terms recap + docs link + kickoff date) and a
   handoff note for onboarding. Log outcome in `crm-hygiene`.

## Rules

- Contracts, legal language, and final pricing always go through the user — the agent drafts,
  the human signs off.
- A lost deal gets a courteous close-out draft and a loss-reason entry; burned bridges cost
  future pipeline.
