## 3. THE AUTOMATED SALES FUNNEL

The website is the sales agent. Every path terminates in a **pre-written WhatsApp message** that arrives structured, so qualification is done before the first human reply.

```
TRAFFIC (FB/IG/WhatsApp broadcast/word of mouth)
   │
   ▼
index.html ── Designs gallery ──► "I want this →" ── WA msg with design name
   │
   ├─ Exploded view (trust/engineering proof)
   │
   ├─ Solar section ──► GESMU site (cross-sell) + solar pre-selected in simulator
   │
   ▼
FINANCING SIMULATOR (model × qty × solar × bank × deposit × term)
   │        └── live monthly payment + rental offset + all-bank comparison
   ▼
"Send this quote to WhatsApp" ──► structured quote lands in Louis's WhatsApp
   │
   ▼
HUMAN CLOSE (CRM: quote → invoice → 30% deposit)
   │
   ▼
Post-delivery: photo request + referral ask + "second unit" bundle offer
```

### Why this converts
1. **Loan simulator = qualification.** A lead that arrives with bank, deposit and term chosen is 10x warmer than "how much?"
2. **Rental-offset line** shows most buyers a *net* monthly cost near zero — the strongest objection killer in the arsenal.
3. **Portfolio section** seeds the multi-unit idea before the first purchase ("one is a home, three is an income").
4. **Bundle discounts (10%/15%)** are visible everywhere, making the second unit feel like lost money if skipped.
5. **Zero backend.** wa.me deep links need no server, no forms, no CRM integration to start. Nothing to break.

### Automation rules (run these, no exceptions)
| Trigger | Action | SLA |
|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp quote received | Reply with formal PDF quote (CRM) | 2 business hours |
